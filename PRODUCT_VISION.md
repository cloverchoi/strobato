# PRODUCT VISION

## Mission

Build the AI synchronization layer for digital music experiences.

## Long-Term Vision

Strobato becomes the infrastructure powering:
- digital sheet music synchronization
- rehearsal systems
- practice intelligence
- music education tools
- ensemble coordination
- adaptive accompaniment
- accessibility tools

## Initial Wedge

Real-time score following.

The SDK:
- listens to live performance
- identifies where the musician is in the score
- automatically turns pages/highlights measures
